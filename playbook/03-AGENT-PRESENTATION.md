## TALKING POINTS (for Q&A)

### "Why can't SAPS just use WhatsApp groups?"
> "WhatsApp is unstructured, unaccountable, and not POPIA-compliant. UNCIP provides a structured workflow with audit trails, verified identities, and role-based access. When a child is found, we know who reported, who responded, and how long it took."

### "What about data privacy?"
> "UNCIP is designed POPIA-compliant from the ground up. Parental consent is required for registration. Children's data is encrypted. Only authorised roles can access specific information. We can self-host within government infrastructure for full data sovereignty."

### "How is this different from Missing Children SA?"
> "Missing Children SA does incredible work, but they operate after a child is reported missing — distributing flyers and social media posts. UNCIP operates in the critical first hour, coordinating the immediate response between the parent, school, and police station."

### "What if parents don't have smartphones?"
> "UNCIP works on any phone with a browser — including basic smartphones. For the pilot, we'll also implement USSD and SMS fallbacks. Community champions at each school can assist parents with registration."

### "Who owns the data?"
> "In a government partnership, the data would be owned by the Department of Basic Education. UNCIP provides the technology platform. We propose a public-private partnership model where the technology is licensed to government, with full data sovereignty."

### "What happens after the pilot?"
> "If the pilot demonstrates impact, we propose integration into DBE's existing LURITS infrastructure. The technology becomes part of the government's digital ecosystem, not a standalone system dependent on external funding."
