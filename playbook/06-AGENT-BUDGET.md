## COST PER CHILD

```
Pilot: R2,400,000 ÷ 2,000 children = R1,200 per child for 6 months
                                     = R200 per child per month

At national scale (12M learners):
  Estimated R25M ÷ 12,000,000 = R2.08 per child per year

Compare to:
  NSFAS per-student cost: ~R80,000/year
  School nutrition programme: ~R1,200/child/year
  UNCIP at scale: R2/child/year
```
